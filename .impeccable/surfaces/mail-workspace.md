# Mail workspace

Mode: Operate. Audience: periodic personal batch cleanup, “understand first, then act with confidence.” Style: PreVibe desktop client default light, selected on 2026-10-06. The 2026-10-07 user-specified AI-first flow supersedes the earlier green concept, collapsible sidebar and dual-start paths. Public screenshots use synthetic fixtures only.

## Surface contract

A 48px minimum header contains brand, account verification, application menu and settings. Permanent 320px analysis sidebar (240px at <=1100px) and continuous mail pane scroll independently; minimum 760px workspace is horizontally accessible in narrow windows. Sidebar content exposes folder scope, every rule checkbox/title/action and consent. The sidebar and rule list remain visible. Choose folders, Mail range and Filtering rules share typography; Analysis settings and Current scope headings are removed. The detailed descriptions are collapsed by default and independently controlled with Expand/Collapse buttons inside each rule. Manage rules is the sole sidebar management entry; adding and editing occur on that page, with complete descriptions visible. Start analysis remains at the sidebar bottom. New rules are prepended through the rule-management top form.

Settings navigation is 配置API, 配置邮箱, 设置语言, 整理规则, 隐私与记录 in that order, defaulting to 配置API. API, mailbox and language use separate pages. Page changes preserve the shared configuration and credential drafts; Save and Return to mail keep their existing semantics. No new routes or services are introduced. The sole “启动分析” reads the configured scope and invokes Jev without modifying mail; selection does not define analysis scope. No separate read-mail or initial ten-message UI. Internal header-only reading remains separate from the current product entry.

Mail uses four tabs: 命中规则, 需确认, 未命中规则, 全部. Matched records remain in their category after operation; review includes unclassified and highest-priority conflicts; non-matched includes preservation judgments with explicit reasons. Valid report load, completed analysis or returning to Matched defaults to executable matches, with manual deselection. Changing other tabs clears selection and search. Changing local subject/sender search removes nonmatching records from the selection; clearing search does not reselect them. Show the selected count without View selected, Clear selection or hidden-selection controls.

The sticky top toolbar always exposes delete, move, archive, junk, read, unread, star and unstar, plus fixed-label “整理”. No selection disables actions. Organize requires Matched, current AI evidence and an executable selected subset; its disabled color is paired with native disabled semantics and a hint. It opens a native dialog, and only explicit confirmation executes. Rule review summarizes actual counts, actions and targets without repeating the main selected-mail list; excluded counts and reasons remain when present. Manual review keeps its per-message plan checks. The modal initially focuses Cancel, makes the background inert and restores trigger focus after Cancel or Escape. Manual plans retain their own target check, confirmation and identity boundaries.

Plaintext reading preserves selection; Escape/close returns to the source button and the mail pane’s scroll position. Focused controls clear the top toolbar. Target changes clear old manual checks while retaining repair controls. AI judgment, execution readiness and actual operation are separate; successful manual action cannot recolor an uncertain judgment.

Analysis metadata contains only time and recorded successful-response token totals, with the accounting caveat in a tooltip. The mail pane shows only destination hints from normal preflight. Failed preflight opens Settings → Organizing rules repair controls; successful save/recheck returns to mail. The bottom privacy text and links are removed from both main surfaces; Privacy & records remains a settings section.

The recent-operation strip shows actual latest-batch progress and recorded destinations. During automatic verification, the strip and affected rows use neutral “confirming” states with no warning or refresh control; already confirmed successes stay successful. Active row scope comes from display-only IDs matched to the operation’s physical mail identity. Reload resumes the progress display. Failed/unknown outcomes disclose that batch’s issues only after verification ends, with saved reasons and read-only refresh. Folder metadata counts are distinct from operation counts, and only the latest batch is undoable. Unknown results cannot be blindly retried or overwritten. Privacy & records explains why verification started and its outcome/duration without recording mail text or account identifiers.

## Style and behavior

PreVibe gray-blue surfaces, deep-gray primary actions, local fonts and thin dividers. Flat mail plane; 10px input, 12px button, 8px row states. Green is brand/success; amber expresses AI uncertainty. Text accompanies color. 160ms transitions respect reduced motion; no optimistic mailbox success.

Scoped autosave preserves stored-rule choices, scope and consent. New unsaved rule IDs stay out of that endpoint until full save or analysis. Full settings preserve existing encrypted storage and consent. Rule priority is delete > junk > move > archive > all flags; highest-level differing actions/targets require confirmation, while keep protection remains. Judgment version 7 invalidates old rule authorization without erasing evidence or undo. No new backend, route framework, DSL, accounts or action chains.
